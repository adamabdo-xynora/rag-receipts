## Double-Wall Hot Cup — Specification

The 12 oz double-wall hot cup is built from a 300 gsm outer board and a 220 gsm inner board with a water-based dispersion lining. It holds liquid at 90 °C without an additional sleeve. Cups are supplied in a carton of 500 across ten sleeves of fifty, so a venue can open one sleeve without exposing the whole carton to humidity.

## Lid Compatibility

The 12 oz cup takes an 80 mm lid. The 8 oz cup also takes an 80 mm lid, which means the two sizes share a single lid line. The 16 oz cup takes a 90 mm lid and shares nothing with the smaller sizes. Ordering the wrong lid diameter is the single most common ordering error on paper goods, so the lid diameter is printed on the carton end panel as well as the label.

## Kraft Carry Bag — Load Rating

The No. 6 kraft carry bag is made from 90 gsm virgin kraft with a twisted paper handle. It is rated to carry 6 kg when dry. The rating assumes an evenly distributed load; a single dense item resting against one gusset will tear the bag well below the rating. Bags are not rated for wet or chilled contents.

## Beverage Napkin — Specification

The 2-ply beverage napkin is 24 cm square folded to a quarter, in unbleached natural or bleached white. Both finishes come from the same base tissue and differ only in bleaching. Napkins are supplied in a carton of 4,000 in bundles of 250.

## Takeout Box — Heat and Sauce Tolerance

The 1,000 mL takeout box is a poly-lined board box with a locking tab lid. It holds hot food at up to 85 °C for one hour and tolerates oil-based sauces. It is not suitable for reheating in an oven, and the locking tab loses its grip once the board has absorbed steam through an unlined fold.
