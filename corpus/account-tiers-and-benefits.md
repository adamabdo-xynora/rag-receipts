## The Three Tiers

Every Larkspur account sits in one of three tiers: Sprout, Harvest, or Orchard. Tier governs the discount applied to list price, the payment terms offered, and the level of service on standing orders. New accounts open in Sprout; the terms and qualification rules for Sprout are set out in the new account onboarding document. Harvest and Orchard are described below.

## Harvest Tier — Discount, Terms, and Qualification

Harvest Tier accounts receive 12% off list price on all stocked dry goods and paper goods, and are offered net 30 payment terms after a clean payment history of three consecutive months. Qualification requires a rolling twelve-week purchase volume of at least $9,000. Harvest accounts may hold one standing order per week and may nominate a preferred delivery window at no charge. Tier is reviewed at the close of each quarter.

## Orchard Tier — Discount, Terms, and Qualification

Orchard Tier accounts receive 18% off list price on all stocked dry goods and paper goods, and are offered net 45 payment terms after a clean payment history of six consecutive months. Qualification requires a rolling twelve-week purchase volume of at least $26,000. Orchard accounts may hold three standing orders per week and may nominate two preferred delivery windows at no charge. Tier is reviewed at the close of each quarter.

## Losing a Tier

A tier is not permanent. If rolling volume falls below the qualification threshold for two consecutive quarterly reviews, the account steps down one tier at the next review. The step-down is communicated in writing fourteen days in advance, and any orders already accepted at the prior tier are honoured at the prior discount.

## What Tier Does Not Change

Tier does not change order minimums, freight thresholds, product specifications, or claim windows. Those are uniform across all accounts. Tier affects discount, payment terms, and standing-order privileges only.
