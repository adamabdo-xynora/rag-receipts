## How Do I Place an Order?

Orders go to orders@example.com or 555-0142 with the account name, the purchase order reference if your venue uses one, and the product codes from the current price list. We do not take orders through a driver, and a note handed to a driver at the door is not an order.

## How Late Can I Order for the Next Delivery?

The ordering cut-off depends on your zone, and your zone is printed on every invoice and packing slip. Cut-off times and standard lead times for each zone are set out in the delivery document. Anything placed after your zone cut-off is treated as a rush order and is subject to the rush surcharge.

## Why Was My Order Held?

Orders are held for three reasons: the order sits below the minimum order value, the account is inside its first three orders and prepayment has not cleared, or the account is on credit hold. A held order is not cancelled. It releases automatically once the cause is resolved, and it keeps its place in the run for its zone.

## Can I Split One Order Across Two Deliveries?

Yes, but each drop is assessed on its own for freight. Splitting an order that would have cleared the free-freight threshold as a single drop usually means paying freight on both drops. Customers who split for storage reasons often do better placing two separate orders timed to different runs.

## What Is the Difference Between a Return and a Claim?

A return is for goods you no longer want; a claim is for goods that arrived damaged, wrong, or short. They have different windows and different evidence requirements, and they are handled by different desks. Do not file a claim to work around a closed return window; it will be declined at assessment.

## Do You Deliver to a Residential Address?

No. Every delivery address must be a commercial premises able to accept a hand-truck or pallet delivery during its nominated window. Home addresses are declined at account setup and cannot be added later as a secondary address.
