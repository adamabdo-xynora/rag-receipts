## Minimum Order Value

The minimum order value for a delivered order is $250 excluding tax. Orders below the minimum are held rather than rejected; the customer is notified and the order releases automatically once additional lines bring it above $250. Depot collection orders have no minimum, but collection must be booked in advance through the ordering desk.

## Free-Freight Thresholds by Zone

Freight is waived when the pre-tax order value reaches the threshold for the account's zone. The thresholds are Zone A $400, Zone B $650, and Zone C $900. Thresholds are evaluated per delivery drop, not per week and not per account. Splitting a single order across two drops means each drop is measured against the threshold on its own.

## How Tier Discounts Interact With List Price

Tier discounts are applied to the list prices above, then freight is assessed against the discounted subtotal. Promotional pricing, where offered, replaces the tier discount rather than stacking with it. The discount percentages for each tier are published in the account tier document, not here, because tiers are reviewed on a different cycle than the price list.

## Price Change Notice

List prices are reviewed quarterly. Customers receive fourteen days' written notice before an increase takes effect. Decreases apply immediately and require no notice.
