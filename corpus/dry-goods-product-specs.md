## Bread Flour — Specification

Larkspur bread flour is milled from hard wheat to a protein content of 12.6% and an ash content of 0.55%. It is supplied in a 25 kg multiwall paper sack with a stitched top and an inner moisture barrier. The sack carries a mill lot code on the side panel; quote that code on any quality query, because it identifies the milling run rather than the delivery.

## Caster Sugar and Fine Sea Salt — Specification

Caster sugar is supplied in a 20 kg sack at a nominal crystal size of 0.35 mm, screened to remove agglomerates. Fine sea salt is supplied in a 10 kg sack, non-iodised, with no anti-caking agent. Because the salt carries no anti-caking agent, it is the one dry good we ask customers to decant into a sealed container on receipt.

## Cocoa Powder — Storage and Shelf Life

Cocoa powder is alkalised to a pH of 7.4 and supplied in a 3 kg lined steel tin. The tin is packed under nitrogen flush and carries a 540-day shelf life from the pack date printed on the base of the tin. Once the seal is broken, the nitrogen flush no longer applies and the powder should be used within 90 days. Shelf life is not extended by refrigeration, and condensation from a cold store will cause clumping.

## Rolled Oats — Specification

Rolled oats are supplied in a 12 kg sack, kilned and rolled to a 0.6 mm flake. They are not gluten-free and are not handled on a segregated line, so they are unsuitable for venues making a gluten-free claim.

## Storage Conditions We Expect

All dry goods should be stored off the floor on a rack or pallet, away from external walls, at a stable ambient temperature and relative humidity below 65%. Larkspur declines quality claims where goods have been stored directly on a concrete floor, because moisture wicks through the paper sack from below.
