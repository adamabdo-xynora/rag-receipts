## Why Hotels and Caterers Are Handled Separately

Hotel and caterer accounts order in bursts tied to events rather than in a steady weekly rhythm. The ordering desk keeps a separate note on each of these accounts recording the loading dock, the goods-in contact, and any building access restriction. These notes are not visible to the customer and are maintained by the ordering desk, not by the account holder.

## Zone B Delivery Windows and Cut-Off

Zone B is served by a corridor van run on each service day. The ordering cut-off for Zone B is 1:00 PM on the service day before delivery, and the standard lead time is two service days. Zone B customers may nominate a morning window of 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM or an afternoon window of 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM. Nominated windows are honoured on a best-effort basis and are not guaranteed against traffic delays.

## Event Holds and Release

A caterer may place an event hold up to eight weeks ahead. A hold reserves stock against the account without generating an invoice and without committing a delivery slot. The hold must be released into a live order before the zone cut-off, or the reserved stock returns to general availability at midnight on the release deadline. Holds are capped at four concurrent events per account.

## Loading Dock and Goods-In Requirements

Deliveries to hotel loading docks are made against a printed goods-in reference supplied by the customer. Drivers will not leave a pallet without that reference. Where a dock requires an induction or a permit, the customer supplies it to the ordering desk at least three service days ahead; drivers cannot arrange access at the door.

## Linen Turnaround for Event Accounts

Event accounts on the linen rental program receive soiled-linen collection at the same visit as the next delivery. Where an event ends more than four service days before the next scheduled delivery, a separate collection may be booked through the linen desk at linens@example.com or 555-0155.
