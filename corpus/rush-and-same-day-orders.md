## What Counts as a Rush Order

An order placed after the zone cut-off for delivery on the next service day is a rush order. An order that asks for delivery on the same service day it is placed is also a rush order, and is accepted only in Zone A. Rush treatment is a scheduling decision made by the depot, not a product option the customer can add to a cart.

## Rush Surcharge

Every accepted rush order carries a surcharge of $38 per drop. The surcharge is charged in addition to any freight that already applies, and it is never waived by reaching a free-freight threshold, because the threshold waives freight rather than handling. Tier discount does not apply to the rush surcharge.

## Same-Day Limits

Same-day requests are accepted in Zone A only, must be received before 9:00 AM, and are capped at eight cartons or one pallet, whichever is smaller. Same-day orders may not include linen rental stock, because that stock sits at the Cedar Hollow depot and cannot be consolidated inside the same-day window.

## Changing an Order After Cut-Off

A line may be added to an order after cut-off only if the order has not yet been picked. Once picking has started, the addition becomes a separate order and is treated as a rush order with its own surcharge. Removing a line after picking is not possible; the line ships and is handled through the returns process instead.

## When a Rush Order Is Refused

The depot may refuse a rush order when the run is already at capacity, when the requested stock is held at the other depot, or when the account is on credit hold. A refused rush order is not charged the surcharge and reverts to the standard lead time for its zone.
