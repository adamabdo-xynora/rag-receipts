## Opening an Account

An account is opened in four steps: submit the trading details form, provide a business address that can accept a pallet or hand-truck delivery, nominate an ordering contact and an accounts contact, and sign the terms acknowledgement. The accounts desk aims to complete setup within two working days of receiving a complete form. Incomplete forms are held, not returned.

## Sprout Tier — Discount, Terms, and Qualification

Sprout Tier accounts receive 5% off list price on all stocked dry goods and paper goods, and are offered net 14 payment terms after a clean payment history of one month. Qualification requires no minimum purchase volume, as Sprout is the entry tier for every new account. Sprout accounts may hold one standing order per fortnight and may nominate a preferred delivery window for a fee of $15 per week. Tier is reviewed at the close of each quarter.

## The First Three Orders

The first three orders on a new account are placed on prepayment regardless of tier. Prepayment is lifted automatically once the third order has been delivered and paid. Customers who need terms sooner may submit a credit application to the accounts desk, which is assessed separately from tier qualification.

## Zone Assignment and What It Fixes

Zone is assigned during onboarding from the delivery address and does not change unless the customer moves premises. Zone determines the delivery schedule, the freight threshold, and whether the account is served by a metropolitan van or the scheduled long-haul run. Customers cannot request a different zone for a cheaper freight threshold.

## Samples Before the First Order

New accounts may request one sample pack at no charge before placing the first order. The sample pack contains one of each stocked paper good and swatches of every linen weave. Dry goods samples are not offered, because we cannot break a sealed sack or tin without voiding its shelf life declaration.
