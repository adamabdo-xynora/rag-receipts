## Return Window and Condition Requirements

Customers may request a return on stocked goods within 14 calendar days of the delivery date. The window is measured from the delivery date printed on the packing slip, not from the invoice date and not from the date the goods were opened. Goods must be unopened, in the original outer carton, and free of price stickers or venue labelling. Requests made after the window closes are declined at the ordering desk and are not escalated.

## What Cannot Be Returned

Custom-printed paper goods, cut-to-order linen, and any item supplied as a special order against a customer's own specification are not returnable at any point. These lines are marked "special order" on the quote and again on the packing slip, so that the customer sees the restriction twice before the goods ship.

## Restocking Fee

Approved returns carry a restocking fee of 15% of the line value. The fee covers inspection, relabelling, and the return of the goods to saleable stock. The fee is deducted from the credit note rather than invoiced separately.

## How a Credit Is Issued

Once returned goods are received and inspected at the Fairwater depot, a credit note is raised against the original invoice. Credit notes are applied to the account balance and are not paid out as cash or card refunds. Where the original invoice has already been settled, the credit sits on the account and is drawn down by the next order.

## Collection of Returned Goods

Returned goods are collected on the account's next scheduled delivery run at no charge. Customers who need a return collected outside a scheduled run pay the redelivery rate for their zone. Goods must be palletised or boxed and available at the same access point used for delivery.
