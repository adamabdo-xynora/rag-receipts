## Response Standards

The customer care desk answers email within one working day and telephone calls within three minutes during staffed hours. Every contact is logged against the account so that a customer never has to repeat the history of an issue to a second agent. Care can be reached at care@example.com or 555-0161.

## Return Window for Stocked Goods

Customers may request a return on stocked goods within 30 calendar days of the delivery date. The window is measured from the delivery date printed on the packing slip. Goods must be unopened, in the original outer carton, and free of price stickers or venue labelling. Care agents are authorised to approve returns inside this window without a supervisor.

## Goodwill Credits

A care agent may issue a goodwill credit of up to $75 per incident without approval, and up to $250 with a team lead's approval. Goodwill credits are recorded separately from claim credits so that quality reporting is not distorted by service gestures. A goodwill credit never replaces a valid damage claim; where both apply, both are issued.

## Escalation Path

An unresolved issue escalates from the care agent to the team lead after two working days, and from the team lead to the depot manager after a further two working days. The customer is told the name of the person now holding the issue at each step. Escalation does not restart any window that is already running against the account.

## Complaints About a Driver or Delivery

Complaints naming a driver or a delivery visit are routed to the depot manager rather than handled by care, because they may require a review of route records. Care confirms receipt to the customer the same day and passes the matter on without offering a resolution, so that the customer is not given two different answers.
