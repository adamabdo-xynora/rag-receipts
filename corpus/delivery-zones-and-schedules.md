## Zone A Delivery Windows and Cut-Off

Zone A is served by a metropolitan van run on each service day. The ordering cut-off for Zone A is 3:00 PM on the service day before delivery, and the standard lead time is one service day. Zone A customers may nominate a morning window of 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM or an afternoon window of 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Nominated windows are honoured on a best-effort basis and are not guaranteed against traffic delays.

## Zone C Freight Surcharge

Zone C sits outside metropolitan van coverage and is served by the scheduled long-haul run. Every Zone C drop carries a flat freight surcharge of $45, applied per drop and not per line or per pallet. The surcharge is waived when the order reaches the Zone C free-freight threshold published in the pricing document. The surcharge is not waived by tier, by order frequency, or by standing-order status.

## Redelivery After a Failed Attempt

If a driver arrives inside the nominated window and no one is present to receive and sign for the goods, the delivery is recorded as a failed attempt and the load returns to the depot. Redelivery is charged at $32 per drop and is scheduled on the next available run for that zone. Chilled or wax-lined goods are not held for redelivery; they are returned to stock and credited.

## Depot Collection

Any account may collect from the Fairwater depot instead of taking delivery. Collections are booked through the ordering desk and are released against the account name and order number. Collection removes freight charges entirely, including the Zone C surcharge. Linen rental stock cannot be collected from Fairwater, as it is held at Cedar Hollow.

## Public Holiday Closures

Both depots close on gazetted public holidays. Orders due on a closed day move to the next service day, and the cut-off moves with them. Standing orders that fall on a closed day are not doubled up; they are simply skipped for that cycle.
