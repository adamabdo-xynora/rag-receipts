## Rental Versus Purchase

Larkspur offers table linen on two commercial models. Rental linen remains Larkspur property, is laundered by Larkspur between uses, and is billed per item per turn. Purchase linen becomes customer property on delivery and is laundered by the customer. The two models draw on the same weaves and colours, so a venue can start on rental and convert to purchase without changing the look of a room.

## The Three Weaves

The Wren weave is a 220 gsm plain weave in natural cotton, used for casual service. The Heron weave is a 260 gsm satin band cotton with a woven hem, used for formal service. The Sable weave is a 190 gsm polycotton blend built for high-turn venues where wrinkle recovery matters more than hand feel.

## Rental Turn Billing

Rental items are billed per turn, where a turn is one delivery of a clean item and one collection of the same item soiled. An item collected clean and unused is not billed. An item that cannot be returned to service after laundering is billed at the replacement value published on the linen rate card.

## Care Requirements for Purchased Linen

Purchased cotton weaves are washed at up to 60 °C and tumble dried on low. The polycotton Sable weave is washed at up to 40 °C and must not be tumble dried, as the blend sets creases permanently under heat. No Larkspur weave should be bleached; bleach breaks the woven hem thread before it lightens the cloth.

## Stains We Cannot Remove

Rental linen returned with candle wax, red wine that has dried for more than 48 hours, or any ink is charged at replacement value rather than at the turn rate. Larkspur does not attempt solvent treatment on rental stock, because a single solvent cycle shortens the usable life of the whole batch it is run with.
