## Who We Supply

Larkspur Supply Co. is a wholesale distributor of dry goods, paper goods, and table linens. Our customers are independent cafes, full-service restaurants, boutique hotels, and off-premise caterers. We do not sell to the general public, and we do not operate a retail counter. Every account is opened under a trading name with a verified business address, and every order ships from a Larkspur depot rather than direct from a mill or converter.

## Depots and Coverage

We operate two depots. The Fairwater depot handles all dry goods and paper goods and holds the majority of our stocked catalogue. The Cedar Hollow depot handles the linen program, including rental stock, and holds a smaller buffer of paper goods for overflow. Orders that draw from both depots are consolidated at Fairwater before dispatch, which adds one working day to the quoted lead time.

## How Service Areas Are Defined

Coverage is described in delivery zones rather than by postcode lists, because our routes are built around depot drive time rather than administrative boundaries. Zone A covers the Fairwater metropolitan area. Zone B covers the Millbrook and Ashgate corridors. Zone C covers outlying towns served by a scheduled long-haul run. Zone assignment is set when the account is opened and is printed on every invoice and packing slip.
